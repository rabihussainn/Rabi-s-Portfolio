Content Moderation and Toxicity Classification

Developed a machine learning model to detect and flag potentially harmful content to enhance content moderation and promote safer online environments on digital platforms.

  • Developed a sophisticated Pretrained Encoder-Transformer model utilizing BERT and DeBERTa architectures for advanced content moderation and toxicity classification.
  • Conducted extensive evaluations and comparisons with alternative models, including shallow classifiers (Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression) and sequence models (RNNs), to ensure superior performance in categorizing online comments.
  • Extracted contextualized text representations using Pretrained Encoder-Transformers for accurate classification across six toxicity categories: mild toxicity, severe toxicity, obscenity, threats, insults, and identity hate.
  • Analyzed model performance through various metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score, highlighting the efficacy and limitations of each approach.
